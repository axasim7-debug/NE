Moscow Murders

  1. Home
  2. Moscow Murders

On November 13, 2022, four University of Idaho students — Madison Mogen, Kaylee Goncalves, Xana Kernodle, and Ethan Chapin — were murdered in their off-campus home in Moscow, Idaho. The case prompted a months-long investigation that involved local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies. On December 30, 2022, Bryan Kohberger was arrested and later charged with four counts of first-degree murder and one count of felony burglary. Following his conviction, the case was fully adjudicated on July 23, 2025. He pled guilty and was sentenced to four fixed life terms to run consecutively without the possibility of parole, as well as a fixed term of 10 years for the burglary.

The investigation generated a substantial volume of records. The police reports are available to the public and can be found at the link below.  

Investigative reports or records produced by other law enforcement or government entities — including the Moscow Police Department, Latah County Sheriff’s Office, Latah County Prosecutor’s Office, Pennsylvania State Police, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Pullman Police Department, University of Idaho, and Washington State University — are maintained by those agencies. Requests for those records should be submitted directly to the respective custodian agency.

These records were created as part of a homicide investigation and may include graphic descriptions, adult content, disturbing images, and sensitive subject matter.

By accessing these records, users acknowledge and accept the potential presence of graphic or sensitive content.

LINK TO PUBLIC RECORDS

Any records not released through the link above must be requested via a public records request.

Some materials may contain redactions to protect the privacy of individuals, including victims, witnesses, and others not charged with a crime.
All records have been reviewed and redacted where appropriate, and in accordance with the Idaho Public Records Act.
