        Defendant Bryan C. Kohberger was previously found to be indigent pursuant to LC. § 19-852

and § 19-854. On June 28, 2023, this Court, based on a stipulation from the parties, entered an Order

for Preparation and Release of Transcript and Record of Grand Jury Proceedings with Conditions.

preparation of the transcript for Defendant’s review, it is ordered that Latah County pay the expense

associated with transcribing the grand jury transcript once the transcript is completed.
