       l. The State has disclosed all items it is aware of relating to “the search and arrest

           have also been given direct access to view and inspect items seized from his

           parents’ residence including his vehicle. The State will continue to disclose

           additional reports it receives that are subject to discovery under I.C.R. l6 and

           applicable case law. To the extent that the Defense believes that certain reports

           may contain “exculpatory information,” the State asks that the Defense specify

           what exculpatory information they are referring to and the basis for their belief
