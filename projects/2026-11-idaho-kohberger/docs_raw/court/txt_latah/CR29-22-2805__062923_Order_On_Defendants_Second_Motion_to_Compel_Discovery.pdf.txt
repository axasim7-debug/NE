       On June 15, 2023, Defendant Bryan C. Kohberger (“Kohberger”) ﬁled Defendant’s Second

Elisa Massoth. The State was represented by William Thompson, Jr., and Ashley Jennings, Latah

       At the outset of the hearing, the parties informed the Court that they had reached agreements

once it was received and reviewed. The defense moved the Court to impose a deadline for the

materials to be turned over to the Court. The parties agreed that July 14, 2023, was reasonable.
