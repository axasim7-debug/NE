        This matter came before the court on February 10, 2023 on the court’s motion to seal or redact

pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(i). The hearing was held via Zoom. William W. Thompson, Jr. and Ashley Jennings

privacy and public disclosure, and announced its ﬁndings of fact 0n the record. Therefore, pursuant to
I.C.A.R. 32(i)(2)(A) and (E) and I.C. §74-124(1)(b), the court ﬁnds it necessary to seal in part and redact
the record related to the search warrant for the following reasons:

        (l) The documents contain highly intimate facts or statements, the publication of which would be
             highly objectionable to a reasonable person; and
        (2) It is necessary to preserve the right to a fair trial.

        l. The Aﬁdavit in Support ofSearch Warrant is SEALED.
        2.   The Search Warrant and Receipt and Inventory be REDACTED.
