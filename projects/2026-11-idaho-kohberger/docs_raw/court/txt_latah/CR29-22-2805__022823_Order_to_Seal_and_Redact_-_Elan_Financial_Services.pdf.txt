        This matter came before the court on February 10, 2023 on the court’s motion to seal or redact

pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(i). The hearing was held via Zoom. William W. Thompson, Jr. and Ashley Jennings

warrant because the documents contain highly intimate facts or statements, the publication of which would
be highly objectionable to a reasonable person. Aﬁer due consideration and with good cause appearing,

        l. The Aﬂidavit in Support ofSearch Warrant is SEALED.
