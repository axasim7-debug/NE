        This matter came before the court on March 14, 2023 on the State’s motion to seal or redact

pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(i). The hearing was held via Zoom. Ashley Jennings appeared on behalf of the State
of Idaho. Anne Taylor appeared on behalf of Mr. Kohberger.
        The court reviewed the records, considered the arguments presented, weighed the interests in

privacy and public disclosure, and announced its ﬁndings of fact on the record. Therefore, pursuant to
I.C.A.R. 32(i)(2)(A) and.(E) and LC. §74-124(l)(b) and (e), the court finds it necessary to seal the records
related to the search warrant for the following reasons:

        (1) The documents contain highly intimate facts or statements, the publication of which would be
            highly objectionable to a reasonable person;
        (2) It is necessary to preserve the right to a fair trial; and
        (3) Production of such records would disclose investigative techniques and procedures.
Aﬁer due consideration and with good cause appearing,
        IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the record herein shall be SEALED until further order of the court.
