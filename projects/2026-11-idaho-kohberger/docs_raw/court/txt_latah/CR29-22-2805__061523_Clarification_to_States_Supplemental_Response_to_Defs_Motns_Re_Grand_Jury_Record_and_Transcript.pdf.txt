       COMES NOW the State Of Idaho, by and through the Latah County Prosecuting

Attorney, and respectfully submits the following clariﬁcation of the State’s position on the

grand jury records/transcript issues:

       The “Defendant’s Reply to State’s Supplemental Response to Defendant’s Motions

Regarding Grand Jury Record and Transcript” dated June 13, 2023, suggests that the defense

believes the State wishes to limit the transcript/record to the evidentiary presentations and list

of grand juror votes (Note: at the bottom of the ﬁrst page of the Defendant’s above-referenced

reply, the defense refers to “the grand juror notes.” Although this is likely a typographical error,

        To clarify, it is the State's position that the record/transcript of the grand jury proceeding

include everything that was done “on the record” and recorded by the clerk and/or court

reporter, including thejury selection process. Were this a situation where the grand jury was “a

standing grand jury” that was not empaneled speciﬁcally for this case, the State would likely

take a different position. However, because this grand jury was empaneled speciﬁcally for the

case at bar, the State believes that the transcript/record should include the jury selection process.

ln essence, the State’s position is that the transcript/record begins when the assignedjudge first


goes on the record and continues for all reported/recorded events through the discharge of the

grand jury. The State respectfully submits that this addresses the Defendant’s interests in the

questioning of prospective jurors as well as the nature and quality of evidence presented

(including whether exculpatory evidence was included) as described by the defense in the first

full paragraph on page 3 of their “Reply.'
