         A public records request was submitted to the Latah County Court Clerk on February 26,

2024, and was subsequently forwarded to this Court for review and a response. The request was to

"examine and/or copy" records pertaining to the "[t]otal cost, to date, of the case against Bryan

Kohberger, including both defense and prosecution, and any other costs this case has required

pursuant to Idaho Code Section 74-102. This response is specific to records of the Latah County

judicial department and, more specifically, records for which this Court is the "custodian judge as

defined in Idaho Court Administrative Rule 32(k)(3)(C).

        First, Idaho Code Section 74-102 does generally give the public the right to examine "any

public record of this state." However, Idaho Code Section 74-104(1)(b) exempts from disclosure

"[rjecords contained in court files of J judicial proceedings, the disclosure of which is prohibited by or

under rules adopted by the Idaho supreme court, but only to the extent that confidentiality is provided

under such rules      ..   .   ." Idaho Court Administrative Rule 32 governs "Records of The Judicial

        Idaho Criminal Rule 12.2 addresses a defendant's ability to file "Motions Requesting

Additional Defense Services." Rule 12.2(d) allows the judge presiding over a case to "request that the

Administrative District Judge appoint another judge to consider and conduct any hearing on the

motion and to decide on the motion." On January 6, 2023, this Court issued an Order Appointing

Resource Judge for this case. Thus, a separate judge is tasked with decisions related to any defense

"motions requesting additional defense services. This Court has no knowledge of the costs associated

with the defense of this case, nor is this Court the "custodian judge" for records pertaining to the "total

cost, to date, of the case against Bryan Kohberger." This is to ensure that any decisions made by the

Court are not in any way influenced by the cost of the case.

        On January 13, 2023, the Resource Judge tasked with evaluating and ruling on any defense

requests for services issued an order Sealing Documents pursuant to Idaho Court Administrative Rule

32(i)(2)(E) "to preserve the right to a fair trial." Therefore, all documents and materials related to

costs associated with the defense's request for services are sealed and exempt from disclosure at this

time. Additionally, the judicial department does not maintain any records related to the costs of the

State's case.
