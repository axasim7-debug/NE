      BRYAN C. KOHBERGER,

                                 Defendant.



        This matter came before the court on March 14, 2023 on the State’s Motion to Temporarily Seal
the Stipulation to Release Property and Related Documents Pending Hearing pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(i).

The hearing was held via Zoom. Ashley Jennings appeared on behalf of the State of Idaho. Anne Taylor

appeared on behalf of Mr. Kohberger.
        The court reviewed the records, considered the arguments presented, weighed the interests in

privacy and public disclosure, and announced its ﬁndings of fact on the record. Therefore, pursuant to
I.C.A.R. 32(i)(2)(A), (D), and (E), and I.C. §74-l24(l)(b), (c), and (e), the court ﬁnds it necessary to seal
in part and redact the records herein for the following reasons:

        (l) The documents contain highly intimate facts or statements, the publication of which would be
             highly objectionable to a reasonable person;
        (2) The documents contain facts or statements that might threaten the safety of or endanger the life
             or safety of individuals;

        (3) It is necessary to preserve the right to a fair trial;
        (4) Disclosure would constitute an unwarranted invasion of personal privacy; and
        (5) Production of such records would disclose investigative techniques and procedures.
After due consideration and with good cause appearing,
        IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the record herein shall be disclosed except for the following:
        l. Exhibit A and B attached to the Stipulation to Release Property are SEALED.
        2. The Stipulation to Release Property and Order to Release Property be REDACTED.

        3.   This order will remain in effect until further order of the court.
