       On May 1, 2023, a coalition of 20 media outlets' (“Associated Press”) ﬁled a Motion to

Intervene and a Motion to Vacate the Amended Nondissemination Order. In opposition to the Motion

to Vacate the Amended Nondissemination Order, Defendant Bryan C. Kohberger ﬁled a Motion to

Take Judicial Notice of Press Coverage.

       Oral argument was heard on June 9, 2023. The State was represented by William W.

Thompson, Jr., and Bradley J. Rudley, Latah County Prosecutor’s Ofﬁce. Defendant was represented

by Anne C. Taylor and Jay W. Logsdon, Kootenai County Public Defender’s Ofﬁce. The Associated

Press was represented by Wendy Olson and Cory Carone.
