         With regard to the following documents, the Court received objection by one or both

    90) 3/31/25 Exhibits to Defendant's 3rd Supplemental Response to Request for Discovery
    RE: Expert Witnesses (Penalty Phase): Within this filing are expert reports and expert
    declarations containing sensitive and detailed confidential mental health information of

    reports and declaration are identified within the filing as Exhibits D7-E; D13-E; D20-B?;

    D20-C, and; D22-B. The release of this information into the public realm would be highly
    objectionable to a reasonable person, which outweighs the public's right to access. Thus, they
    shall be redacted from the filing pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(i)(3)(A)(1).
