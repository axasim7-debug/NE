Forensic DNA is usually lower quality
• Less DNA available

• DNA may be a mixture of more than one person

• DNA may be degraded

• Non-human DNA contamination may be present
“Next generation” genome sequencing can help


    Prep               Sequence                               Analyze

Next generation sequencing “skims” the DNA
NGS kits are modified via bioinformatics
• Fill in missing SNPs using statistics (imputation)


• Strip out SNPs not used by the genealogy databases to mimic
  a microarray file


• Once the kit is uploaded and processed, the genealogy steps
  are the same as described earlier
