       As part of the process to review sealed records to determine which should be unsealed, the
Court will process the review of those documents from newest to oldest. The Court will require
the parties to indicate whether they have an objection to unsealing any specific document and if

they believe a document should remain sealed, they must identify why redacting of materials is
not an acceptable arrangement and with detail and specificity why the document should be sealed,

from both a legal and factual standpoint. Simply citing a court rule without more is not a sufficient

response. The response must identify each document by date, name and number of each document
identified in the order for review of sealed status of a particular group of documents. The parties
must file their response, including if appropriate, a notice of non-objection, within fourteen (14)

days of the Court's order identifying a particular group of documents for review of sealed status.
