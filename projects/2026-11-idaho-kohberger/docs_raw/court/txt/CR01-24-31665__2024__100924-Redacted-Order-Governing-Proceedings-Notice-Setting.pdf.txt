Any prior scheduling order is hereby VACATED. The following schedule will govern the

pretrial matters outlined herein and the trial in this case:

      A. Voir Dire and Trial Setting

                Jury questionnaires will be administered Approximately [REDACTED] 2025.

                Voir dire shall commence on July 30, 2025 at 9:00 a.m.


                The jury trial shall commence on August 11, 2025, and run through November 7,
                2025, inclusive of the penalty phase, if necessary. Trial will begin each day at
                8:30 a.m. and conclude at approximately 3:30 p.m. with a lunch break of
                approximately 45 minutes, Monday through Friday. The parties should be aware
                that some trial weeks may need to be shortened depending on other obligations of
                the court. Counsel should have sufficient evidence/witnesses ready to go each day
                to utilize the full day's trial schedule.


                A final pre-trial conference will be held on May 15, 2025, at 9:00 a.m. and
                continue May 16, 2025, if necessary. Earlier status conferences may be requested
                of the Court and/or initiated by the Court.

       D. Trial Materials

                e   Proposed jury questionnaires shall be simultaneously filed under seal by March
                    24, 2025. Objections are due March 31, 2025. A sealed/closed hearing on the
                    proposed jury questionnaires will be held on April 16, 2025.

                e   Proposed exhibit lists shall be filed and exchanged by April 21, 2025. The parties
                    must identify exhibits specifically and individually, with each exhibit referenced
                    by the Bates number used during discovery."

                e   Copies of Exhibits shall be exchanged by April 21, 2025. Each exhibit must be
                    affixed with an exhibit number. State shall use red exhibits stickers beginning
                    with S-1. The defense shall use blue stickers beginning with D-1. To expedite

                    determination made as to whether an objection will be lodged against the exhibit

                    shall jointly file a log (spreadsheet) identifying which exhibits are objected to
                    (and the basis of the objection) and those to which there is no objection.

                e   Guilt phase lay witness lists shall be filed under seal by April 21, 2025. Penalty
