Field Notes:
On 11/16/22 at approximately 1912 PST, Forensic Scientists (FS) Kerry Hogan, Hailey Youngling, Jennie
Ayers, Wyatt Barie and I arrived at the Idaho State Police District 2 (ISP D2) office located at 2700 Old N
S Hwy, Lewiston, Idaho 83501, to assist with a homicide investigation. Detective Talbott (ISP D2) was
present upon our arrival. Detective Talbott briefed us on the scene information.


1122 King Rd.

On 11/17/22 at approximately 0854 PST, FS Hogan, Youngling, Ayers, Barie and I arrived at 1122 King Rd,
Moscow, Idaho 83843. Detective Talbott was present upon our arrival. 1122 King Rd is a white colored
three story home that faces north in a residential neighborhood. The house consists of 6 bedrooms and
3 bathrooms. The first floor consists of a west bedroom, a bathroom, a furnace room, an east bedroom
and a stairway to the second floor. The second floor consists of a living room, a bathroom, a west
bedroom, an east bedroom, a kitchen, a storage room under the stairs and a switchback staircase to the
third floor. The third floor consists of a west bedroom, a landing, a bathroom and an east bedroom.

The second floor west bedroom had few belongings. A bed was against the south wall and a table with
two drawers was against the north wall. A freestanding mirror and furry chair were in the northwest
corner of the room. There were a number of apparent bloodstains on the bed, the south wall, the west
wall, the door and the floor.

In the third floor east bedroom a bed was against the north wall and a closet was against the south wall.
A desk was against the west wall. There were a number of apparent bloodstains on the bed, the north
wall, the east wall, the west wall and the door.

FS Barie and Ayers took photographs of the scene using the LizQ Imaging System.

1127 King Rd.

On 11/18/22 at approximately 2117 PST, FS Hogan arrived at 1127 King Rd. Moscow, Idaho 83843, to
assist in the search of an apartment. FS Hogan was accompanied by Detective Talbott and Sergeant Van
Leuven (ISP D1).

1127 King Rd is an apartment building consisting of two stories. Apartment “1” is located on the ground
level and is a multi-bedroom apartment with two bathrooms, a living room and a kitchen. There is a
hallway with a kitchen to the north and a living room area to the north of the kitchen. There is at least
two bedrooms east of the kitchen. There are two bathrooms south of the kitchen, on the east side of
the apartment. The response was limited to the southeast bedroom.

FS Hogan performed two presumptive tests for the presence of blood on the south wall of the entry
hallway (marker 1) and on the east wall of the kitchen (marker 2) using Ortho-Tolidine. Positive results,
indicating the presence of blood, were obtained on both stains. Swabs were collected from markers 1
and 2 by FS Hogan.

FS Hogan cleared at approximately 2224 PST.

All collected swabs were turned over to Detective Talbott on 11/18/22 at approximately 2241 PST.

We transferred digital images to Detective Talbott on 11/19/22 at approximately 0838 PST.

1630 NE Valley Rd. #G201

Field Notes:

On 12/30/22 at approximately 0705 PST, Forensic Scientists (FS) Hailey Youngling, Jennie Ayers and I
responded to 1630 NE Valley Rd, #G201, Pullman, Washington to assist in a homicide investigation.
Jeffory Talbott with Idaho State Police District 2 (ISP D2) and Dustin Blaker with Moscow Police
Department arrived to the residence with us. Deputy Chief Dawn Daniels with Washington State
University Police Department (WSU PD) was present upon our arrival. The scene was not secured at this
time. The temperature was approximately 33 degrees Fahrenheit and the weather was snowy and wet.

1630 NE Valley Rd #G201 is a beige six-plex apartment building with a maroon/brown colored “G” on
the exterior. Apartment #G201 is on the second floor on the east side of the building. The second floor
staircase is flush with the surrounding sidewalk and has a single flight of stairs going up. The entryway
to apartment #G201 is directly east of the staircase.

The interior of apartment #G201 was well kept and sparsely furnished. There are two bedrooms, one
bathroom with a separate water closet, a living room and a kitchen. The entryway door enters to the
living room. The living room was furnished with a two-seat sofa on the east wall. There was a coffee
table and a rug in front of the sofa. On the west wall, there was a television stand with a television and
various books on the interior shelving. In the southwest corner of the living room, there was a
disconnected air conditioning type unit. The south wall of the living room has a door that opens to the
exterior patio. To the north of the television stand, there was a table with one lamp, powered off. On
the north wall of the living room, there is a closet. East of the closet (on the north side of the
apartment) are two bedrooms, one on the west side, one on the east side. The northwest bedroom was
unfurnished and was locked prior to entry. There are two empty 2-door closets located on the west
                                                  Page 1 of 3



                                                009323
Laboratory Case Number: M2022-4843                                                        Report No.: 25


wall. The door to the northeast bedroom is in the northwest corner of the room. Along the west wall
there was a table that contains a computer tower and electronics. A bed was against the north wall.
The east wall contains two 2-door closets. To the south of the closet, there was a stool with folded
white towels, a small white trash bin and a white laundry basket. The bathroom is located to the south
of the northeast bedroom. The kitchen is located to the south of the bathroom. There was a fridge in
the northwest corner of the kitchen. To the east of the fridge is a sink. The stove and oven was on the
east wall. There were various kitchen appliances on the counter including a dishrack, an electric can
opener, a microwave, coffee makers and a bean grinder. The kitchen cabinets contained various food
items, dishes, cooking utensils, pots and pans. On the south wall of the kitchen, there was a table with
shelving and one stool.

I photographed the exterior and interior of the apartment for overall location, identification and
condition.

FS Ayers captured images using the LizQ Imaging System.

FS Ayers performed presumptive tests for the presence of blood on 51 areas inside the apartment.
Positive results, indicating the presence of blood, were obtained on two stains from the bedding of the
northeast bedroom. A cutting was collected from a pillow in the northeast bedroom (marker B) and a
mattress cover was collected from the bed in the northeast bedroom (marker C). A third stain (marker
A) was collected from the kitchen countertop but was not tested, due to the size and condition.

Blood was not detected on twelve stains tested in the kitchen, fifteen stains tested in the northeast
bedroom, seventeen stains tested in the bathroom, and five stains tested in the living room.

I collected eleven items from the apartment. I collected five items from the north wall closet: one nitrile
type black colored glove (marker 1), one “Walmart” receipt with one “Dickies” tag (marker 2), two
“Marshalls” receipts (marker 3), one dust canister from “Bissell PowerForce” vacuum (marker 4), eight
possible hair strands from the head of the “Libman” broom (marker 5). I collected one “FireTV” stick
with cord and plug from the living room television (marker 6). I collected one possible hair strand from
the east wall in the bathroom (marker 8) and one possible hair strand inside the shower drain in the
bathroom (marker 9). I collected three items from the northeast bedroom: one possible animal hair
strand (marker 7), one possible hair strand from the computer pad in the northeast bedroom (marker
10), and one possible hair strand from a pillow in the northeast bedroom (marker 11). FS Ayers
collected one computer tower in the northeast bedroom (marker 12).

We cleared the scene at approximately 1933 PST.

All evidence was turned over to Deputy Chief Daniels at approximately 2022 PST on 12/30/22.
