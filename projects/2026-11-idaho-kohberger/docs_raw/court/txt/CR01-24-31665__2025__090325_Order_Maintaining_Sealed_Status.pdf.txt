       The Court finds that the sealed status of the following records identified in the Court's

August 4, 2025, Order Regarding Sealed Documents shall remain in effect: 9, 18, 25, 26, 27, 30,

31, 35 and 40.

       Item 9 must remain sealed as it is replete with identifying information of the proffered

alternate perpetrators, the unsealing of which would contravene the privacy concerns of these

individuals and likely subject them to unwarranted harassment and reputational harm. Thus,

continued sealing is warranted pursuant to I.C.A.R. 32(4)(3)(A)(1) and (7). The remaining

identified records all relate to an ongoing and statutorily confidential investigation into potential

leaks in this case and are exempt from disclosure under I.C.A.R 32(g)(1) and LC. § 74-124(a).



IT IS SO ORDERED.
