       By this Order, the Court directs that current defense counsel, Anne Taylor, Elisa Massoth

and Jay Logsdon, shall remain as counsel of record for Defendant unless they are relieved by a


subsequent order of this Court. An ex-parte, sealed/closed hearing will be held on Tuesday,

Public Defender, Eric Frederickson, must appear at the hearing as well.
