        Venue in this case has been changed to Ada County pursuant to order of the Idaho Supreme

Court. This order is intended to clarify how records in the case may be accessed or requested

pursuant to Idaho Court Administrative Rule (I.C.A.R.) 32.

        Records that are available to the public may be accessed at the Idaho Supreme Court's

Cases of Interest site: https://www.isc.idaho.gov/.

        Requests for audios of hearings are to be submitted to the Ada County TCA Transcripts

Department: transcripts@adacounty.id.gov, even if those hearings took place in Latah County.

        Requests for sealed records are to be submitted to the Ada County Clerk's Office. The

sealed records request form is available at:

        https://adacounty.id.gov/clerk/courts/forms-and-applications/.

        The completed sealed records request form may be emailed to:

        criminalrecordsrequest@adacounty.id.gov.
