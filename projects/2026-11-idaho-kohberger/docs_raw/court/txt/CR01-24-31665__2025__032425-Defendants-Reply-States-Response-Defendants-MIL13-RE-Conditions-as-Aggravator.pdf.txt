       The State has conceded it agrees with Mr. Kohberger’s request in his motion to prevent the

state from using his diagnosis and conditions thereof as aggravators in support of death should the

case reach that stage. Mr. Kohberger’s request finds basis in the caselaw cited in his Motion in

Limine. The State’s cited caselaw also supports his Motion that his diagnosis and conditions of

autism not be used as aggravating factors.

       Mr. Kohberger has moved for, and it is unrefuted, that the State not use in support of

aggravation, his diagnosis and characteristics of autism. The court should grant the motion as

requested.
