      V. ATTITUDE REGARDING THE DEATH PENALTY
      -   Question 50, the Defense requests that the question include the below additional
          language:
             “If the defendant is found guilty of first-degree murder, it is possible that the jury
             will have to decide whether or not to impose life without parole or the death
             penalty. Before the jury….”
      -   Question 53, the Defense requests that the question include the below additional
          language in section d. and g.

           “d. While I generally favor the death penalty, I can vote against imposing it if the
               State has not met its burden of proof that the death penalty should be imposed
               based on the facts, or if mitigating evidence presented convinces me life
               without parole is approriate, law, and Judge’s instructions given in the case.”
           “g. While I generally oppose the death penalty, I can vote to impose it if the State
               has met its burden of proof that the death penalty should be imposed based on
               the facts, no mitigating evidence convinces me life without parole is
               appropriate, law and Judge’s instructions given in the case.”

      -   Question 55, the Defense requests that the question include the following additional
          attorneys:
                 • Ingrid Batey
                 • Madison Allen

      -   Question 61, the Defense objects - The coverage of this case has been pervasive. It is
          in the media every day. There has been a lot of misinformation throughout the
          pendency and any prospective juror that has read, heard or watched the coverage
          cannot set aside what they have read, seen or heard. Bias runs deep and strong
          depending on many potential connections to the case or to stories read seen or heard.
          The change of venue was granted for this reason.

      -   Question 62, the Defense requests that the question be reworded to include the below
          additional language:

             “If you do not hear evidence during trial matching what you have heard from
             media or other sources prior to the trial (or hear evidence that differs from what
             you heard from sources outside of trial), how will that impact the evidence
             presented during trial when deciding about the case as a juror?”
