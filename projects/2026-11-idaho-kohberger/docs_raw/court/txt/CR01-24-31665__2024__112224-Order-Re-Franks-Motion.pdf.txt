        Before the Court is Defendant's Motion for Frank's Hearing (Nov. 14, 2024) and

Memorandum in Support of Motion for Frank's Hearing (Nov. 18, 2024). Accompanying the

motion are 38 exhibits comprising over 2000 pages. Unfortunately, Defendant's memorandum

largely fails to identify with particularity the relevant portions of the exhibits, instead referring

simply to the exhibit number without identifying the precise pages at issue                  Thus, the Court is

record looking for evidence." Venable v. Internet Auto Rent & Sales, Inc., 156 Idaho 574, 582,

329 P.3d 356, 364 (2014).

memorandum identifying the relevant portions of the record by page number (and line number if
