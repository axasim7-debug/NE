       COMES NOW, Bryan C. Kohberger, by and through his attorneys of record, and hereby

responds to the State’s Motion to Amend the Order of Restitution filed with the Court on March

19, 2026. Pursuant to Idaho Code 19-5304(6) restitution remained open for 60 days after Mr.

Kohberger was sentenced on July 23, 2025. The State now seeks to amend the order to correct a

recipient of restitution. Mr. Kohberger would note that this correction became necessary only after

the expiration of the extended period granted by the Court. Mr. Kohberger questions the Court’s

jurisdiction to amend the restitution order, but defers to the Court’s wisdom.
