30    46. In this case, the State has filed a notice of intention to seek the death penalty. This means
31   that if, and only if, Mr. Kohberger is found guilty of first degree premeditated murder there will
32   be a separate trial known as a sentencing proceeding. During this sentencing proceeding, other
33   evidence may be presented to the jury. You are not to draw any conclusions about why you are
34   being asked questions about the death penalty before this trial has commenced. Do not think that


                                                                                                      PAGE 12
                                                                                       RED Card # ______
 1   anyone has determined that a sentencing proceeding will be required or that Mr. Kohberger will
 2   be found guilty of any offense. This is very important.

 3   If the defendant is convicted of first degree murder, the same jury that determined guilt may be
 4   asked to determine the appropriate sentence in a separate proceeding. In the sentencing phase of
 5   the case, the prosecution is required to establish the existence of an aggravating circumstance(s),
 6   which the prosecution asserts support the death penalty. On the other hand, the defense will
 7   present mitigating circumstances to the jury, which the defense asserts support a penalty of life
 8   imprisonment. Aggravating circumstances are factors set out by statute and alleged by the State
 9   in support of a death sentence. Mitigating circumstances are anything about the defendant or the
10   facts of the case that, in fairness or mercy, indicate that a sentence other than death is
11   appropriate. If you find the defendant guilty, the alternative to the death penalty is a sentence of
12   life imprisonment.
