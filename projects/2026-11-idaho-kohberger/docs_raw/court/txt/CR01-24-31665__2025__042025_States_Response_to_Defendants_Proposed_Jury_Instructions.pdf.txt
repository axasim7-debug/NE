                                    INTRODUCTION

       The State does not object to Defendant's Proposed Jury Instructions, which are model

instructions provided in the Idaho Supreme Court’s Idaho Criminal Jury Instructions (ICJI) and

are also proposed by the State in its Requested Jury Instructions. To the extent it is helpful to the

cross-references them to the State’s Requested Jury Instructions, and notes any differences.

                                           RESPONSES

ICJI 103

       Defendant’s proposal deviates from the model ICJI 103 provided by the Idaho Supreme

Court. The State does not object to Defendant’s modification, however, because it tracks the

language of ICJI 1703, which is the burden of proof instruction to be given in the death penalty

phase. The State included ICJI 1703’s language in its Proposed Preliminary Jury Instruction No. 1

(which relies on ICJI 001 and 1703). The State submits that it is appropriate for the Court to give

a version of ICJI 1703 pre-trial, post-trial, and during the penalty phase.

ICJI 318

       The State agrees that ICJI 318 should be given if applicable based on testimony during

trial. The State listed ICJI 318 as its Proposed Post-Proof Instruction No. 22.

ICJI 319

       The State agrees that ICJI 319 should be given if applicable based on testimony during

trial. The State listed ICJI 319 as its Proposed Post-Proof Instruction No. 23.

ICJI 345

       The State agrees that ICJI 345 should be given if applicable based on testimony during

trial. The State listed ICJI 345 as its Proposed Post-Proof Instruction No. 26.
