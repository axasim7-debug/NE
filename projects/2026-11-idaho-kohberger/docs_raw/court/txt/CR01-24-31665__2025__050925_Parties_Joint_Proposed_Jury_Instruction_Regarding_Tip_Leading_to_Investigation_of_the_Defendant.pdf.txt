                                       INTRODUCTION

       In its April 18, 2025, Order Memorializing Oral Rulings on Motions in Limine, the Court

advised the parties to meet and confer regarding a jury instruction to explain the "tip" that caused

law enforcement to investigate Defendant.       The parties have agreed on the proposed jury

instruction below, which relates to the testimony of Moscow Police Department Corporal Brent

Payne.

         The State intends to ask foundational questions of Corporal Payne to elicit the testimony

addressed in the proposed instructions. Specifically, the State intends to ask (i) whether law

enforcement received tips that it investigated between November 13, 2022, and December 19,

2022, (ii) if any of those tips had been substantiated, and (iii) if law enforcement received a tip on

December 19, 2022, that caused them to investigate Defendant.

         Defendant does not stipulate to parts (i) and (ii) of the anticipated testimony and reserves

the right to cross-examine Corporal Payne about that testimony but will not address Investigative

Genetic Genealogy (“IGG”).

                               PROPOSED JURY INSTRUCTION

PARTIES’ JOINT PROPOSED INSTRUCTION NO. 1

         You heard Corporal Payne testify that the investigation team received a tip on December

19, 2022, that caused them to investigate the defendant, Bryan C. Kohberger.

         This testimony was admitted for the limited purpose of explaining what caused law

enforcement to investigate the defendant. You are not to consider this testimony for any purpose

other than the limited purpose of when and why law enforcement first investigated the defendant.

You are not to speculate as to the source or substance of the tip.
