                                                  FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION




                                                                                                                          Date of entry       07/17/2024



        In the early morning hours of July 17, 2024 SA Hille responded to the area
        of 1630 NE Valley Road Unit G201, Pullman, WA which is the former residence
        of the subject of this investigation, Bryan KOHBERGER.

        The purpose of the visit to this area was to recreate drive time from
        KOHBERGER's residence to the intersection of WA 270 E and Airport Road. At
        this location a video camera from Floyd's Cannabis captured a white sedan
        traveling southeast through this intersection at approximately 3:02 a.m. on
        November 13, 2022.

        SA Hille proceeded to drive from 1630 NE Valley Road to the intersection of
        WA 270 E and Airport Road via two different routes which are detailed below.
        SA Hille, while driving the routes, utilized cruise control in order to
        ensure the posted speed limit was maintained throughout the entirety of the
