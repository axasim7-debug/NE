       COMES NOW the State of Idaho, by and through the Latah County Prosecuting

Attorney, and respectfully replies to the “Defendant’s Objection to State’s Motion in Limine Re:

911 Call” filed on March 17, 2025.

       Defendant relies on DM’s and BF’s actions from the night before to support his argument

that the statements on the 911 call cannot be excited utterances or present sense impressions.

This is misplaced. The excitement leading up to and during the 911 call was not caused solely

from seeing a male in the home the night before (while that was assuredly a startling event), but

from discovering moments earlier that their roommate and/or friend was not waking up. This is

in addition to also realizing that none of the other victims in the home were responding. The

entire call should be admitted through the excited utterance exception to the rule against hearsay.

admitted as each statement is either an excited utterance, a present sense impression, or is non-
