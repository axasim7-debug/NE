3.        Narrative

On December 8, 2022, I was asked to follow-up on a tip submitted to the FBI from
an unknown person in Kitsap County. The tip provided a male in his 60's was
seen trying to entice children in Kingston WA and he was driving a white Hyundai
Elantra with Washington plate           The report also stated the male possibly
worked or hung out near the ARCO gas station in Kingston WA. The tip also stated
the male had reported he was thinking of moving to Moscow Idaho.
I contacted a Deputy Aaron Logdahl and asked him if anyone had made a report to
them from 2021 about an older male trying to entice children. Deputy Logdahl
advised me they did have a report and stated the case was closed and he did not
believe they ever identified a suspect in the case.

The plate returned to a C     or J       B       from                  WA.
Deputy Logdahl provided me with this info and then told me he would go to ARCO
gas station and do some follow-up.
Deputy Logdahl called me back a short time later and told me he talked to
several employee's at the ARCO and none of them remember any older male matching
that description or anyone who drove a white Hyundai Elantra.
Deputy Logdahl provided me with a report number in case I needed it.
On August 6th I received an email for discovery and it was asking if we had any
reports from this tip. Once I remembered what the tip was and what I did with
it I realized I never called Kitsap County back to get a copy of the report.
On August 8, 2024, I called Kitsap County and received a copy of Deputy
Logdahl's agency assist report he completed for his assistance with me and I
also received a copy of the original report submitted to Kitsap County on April
15, 2021.

No suspect was ever identified in the case from Kitsap County. Nothing from
this tip lead us to believe it was related to this homicide case.

I have electronically attached both of these cases to this report.
