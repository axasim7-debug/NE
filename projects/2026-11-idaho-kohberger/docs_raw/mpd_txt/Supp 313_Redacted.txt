Additional Amazon Purchases Located in Kohberger's Vehicle:

following items, which were on his Amazon purchase history, ordered on
06/20/2022. I previously looked up all of the items ordered and had a photo of
what the products looked like:
- Leadbrand Scissor Jack 1.5 tons (3,307 lbs) Capacity Ratchet Handle Saving
Strength Design
- EP Auto 14" Heavy Duty Universal Lug Wrench, 4-Way Cross Wrench
- Dorman 926-021 Spare Tire for Select Hyundai/Kia Models

- Worksite Tire Inflator Air Compressor Portable, 20V cordless tire pump 150 PSI
with Digital pressure gauge, rechargeable Li-ion battery and 12V car power
Adapter
- Orcish 70 Pcs Tire repair kit, heavy duty tire plug kit flat tire repair kit
tire patch kits puncture repair kit (70pcs)
- Cartman Warning Triangle DOT approved, identical to: United States FMVSS
571.125, reflective warning road safety triangle kit, pack of 3.
T   S          , MPD Evidence Technician, unlocked the secured garage, where
Kohberger's Hyundai Elantra was being stored. I took photos of the vehicle and
the intact evidence seals, prior to entering the vehicle. I opened the driver's
side door and opened the trunk, using the trunk release lever, breaking both the
driver's side door and trunk evidence seals. I took photos of the trunk and the
items in the trunk. I removed all of the items from inside of the trunk. I
located the following items in the trunk, which matched the Amazon purchased
items, and took photos of them:
- No Brand, 70 Pcs Tire repair kit, heavy duty tire plug kit flat tire repair
kit tire patch kits puncture repair kit (70pcs), in a black case.
- Cartman warning triangle DOT approved, identical to: United States FMVSS
571.125, reflective warning road safety triangle kit, pack of 3, in a red
plastic case.
- No Brand, Scissor Jack 1.5 tons.

I located a brand new spare tire in the trunk which did not have a brand name on
it. There was a barcode sticker on the back of the rim with the product number
926-016. I searched the Internet for this item number (926-016) and was
directed to Partsgeek.com, Dorman Spare Tire- 2011-2017 Hyundai Accent. I
searched the Internet for Dorman 926-021 Spare Tire for 2011-2019 Hyundai
Elantra and located a photo of the product, on Partsgeek.com. Both 926-016 and
926-021 photos, from Partsgeek.com, were identical to the spare tire located in
the trunk. I took multiple photos of the spare tire and left it in the trunk.

I did not find the following items in the trunk:
- (3,307 lbs) Capacity Ratchet Handle Saving Strength Design (purchased with the

- EP Auto 14" Heavy Duty Universal Lug Wrench, 4-Way Cross Wrench
- Worksite Tire Inflator Air Compressor Portable, 20V cordless tire pump 150 PSI
