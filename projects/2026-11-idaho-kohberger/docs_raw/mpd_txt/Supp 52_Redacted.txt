3.        Narrative

At approximately 1700 hours on 11/24/22, I took the MPD school car, a silver
Ford Fusion, and drove the route of the suspect vehicle. I drove two separate
routes in front of the camera located at      Ridge Road, once westbound on
Lauder Avenue and then twice on Ridge Road, southbound and northbound. I advised
the command post of the times of these runs and requested the video from the
point of contact at      Ridge Road in order to compare the color of the test
vehicle to that of the suspect vehicle.
Once I completed these runs, I drove to 1122 King Road and walked the route
M     M          stated she took while on foot near the house on 11/13/22. I
also parked the MPD school car on Queen Road immediately to the east of 1122
King Road in order to observe the effects of the orange-tinted street lights
located in the area. The lights appeared to make the silver vehicle look tanner
in color than it would under white street lights.

I also walked down and spoke to OFC Tesdahl who was providing scene security at
this time. I spoke to OFC Tesdahl for a moment and then began to walk back to my
patrol vehicle. As I did so, I noticed a black glove laying in the parking lot
approximately 25 feet from the front door. The glove was laying palm-up and
appeared to have been laying in that spot for a couple of days as it did not
have any snow on it. I nudged the glove with the toe of my boot and found it was
frozen to the ground. I asked OFC Tesdahl for rubber gloves and his flashlight.
Upon further examination, there appeared to be a dark, fluid like substance on
the glove. This substance's color and consistency was consistent with dried
blood. OFC Tesdahl provided me with an evidence bag and I collected the glove as
evidence. The glove is a "Polar Penguin" brand glove with a rubberized palm and
fingers. This type of glove is often used for concrete work. I brought the glove
back to MPD and booked it into MPD evidence for submission to the state lab.
The videos of the vehicle test runs were later collected and added to the FBI
video files for this case.
