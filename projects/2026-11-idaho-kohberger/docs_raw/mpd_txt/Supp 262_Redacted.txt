On Monday, March 11, I received an email from PA Thompson advising another Latah
County resident had received the same call. I began to investigate the origin of
these calls and discovered the following. The business appeared to be a
legitimate survey company out of Alabama called Research Strategies Inc. Their
website, https://researchstrategiesinc.com/, stated the company specialized in
public opinion surveys and post-trial jury analysis among many other types of
phone surveys. The phone number used to call Ms. M      shows on the Better
Business Bureau website for the same company out of Alabama. I checked TLO and
found the number used to be associated with a woman in Alabama who passed away
in
2011 which further lends to it being the correct number, just reassigned to this
business.

I spoke to Mrs. M      on Monday, 3/11, and she stated she received the call at
approximately 4:51 pm the day prior. The first question she was asked was if she
was a Latah County resident. There were several questions that followed which
asked generic questions about trials and the criminal justice system. Then she
stated the woman asked her specifically if she was aware of the Kohberger trial.
Mrs. M      stated she refused to answer, asked the woman who she worked for
(Research Strategies, with the number provided), who was paying for the survey,
and asked for her supervisor. The woman gave her the company's number and stated
she was unaware of who was responsible for the survey. Mrs. M      also stated
the number the woman called from was             . This number is not found in
TLO and is likely a Google voice type number not associated with a person.
Mrs. M      stated she posted to her Facebook page asking if anyone else had
received similar calls. She stated several people in the Potlatch area had
received calls from the same number that called her but most were not answered.
I asked her to keep me posted if she became aware of any further information. I
later learned six people on her Facebook post from the Potlatch area had
received a call from the same number. Additionally, Mrs. M      stated the call
made her very uncomfortable and she did not think it was right for people to be
calling and asking such questions.
