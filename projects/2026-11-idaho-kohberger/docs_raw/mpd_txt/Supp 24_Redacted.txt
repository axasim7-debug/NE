Twitch Video

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 11/18/2022

On November 13, 2022, I was informed two of the victims had been patrons at the
Grub Truck, located on Main Street in Moscow, Idaho. I was also informed Grub
Truck streams live feeds of their customers and sales via Twitch.tv.
I was able to locate the Twitch account "GrubTruckers" and locate the stream
from the November 12-13, 2022. This stream was labelled, "The Carbonara
effect... we'll never reveal our secret ingredient" and located at
https://www.twitch.tv/videos/1651413498.

In the video, Kalyee Goncalves and Maddie Mogen are seen walking into camera
view on the video at 3:43:01 timestamp. They are accompanied by a male, later
identified as J    "J   " S        . Goncalves orders and receives food.
At the 3:53:31 timestamp, the girls are seen walking around the truck and leave
the area. S         appears to be talking to another patron and does not see the
girls leave. S         then raises his arms in a "What?" fashion and walks away
in a different direction than the girls.

A copy of this video was captured and saved as evidence.
