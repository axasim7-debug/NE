Meeting with Spokane County Medical Examiner:

On June 7, 2023, I reached out to Dr. V     S    , Chief Medical Examiner for
the Spokane County Medical Examiner's Office, to arrange a meeting with the
Latah
County Prosecutor's team. Dr. S      stated she could meet on June 28, 2023,
which worked for the Prosecuting Team.

On June 27, 2023, I met with T   S          , MPD Evidence Technician, and had
item # 733 (23M-1025), Ka Bar knife, temporarily released to me so I could bring
it to the meeting with Dr. S    . I locked item #733 into a temporary lock box
in MPD Detectives, as our meeting was on the following day.
On June 28, 2023, I removed item #733 from the lock box in MPD Detectives and
brought it with me to Spokane.
On June 28, 2023, at approximately 1030 hours, myself, along with Bill Thompson,
Ashley Jennings, Jeff Nye, and Ingrid Batey met with Dr. S     at the Spokane
County Medical Examiner's Office. Dr. S      had pre-selected several photos,
from each victim, to go over with us so she could describe the shape of a weapon
which would have caused the injuries. I removed the Ka Bar knife from the
sealed package and showed it to Dr. S    . Dr. S      stated, the shape and size
of the Ka Bar knife was consistent as being the weapon which could have caused
the injuries on all four of the victims.
Dr. S     described how some of the wounds had two points on either end and how
some wounds had a point on one end and a square shape at the other end. Dr.
S     explained how the Ka Bar knife could have created these wounds, depending
on the depth of the injury. Dr. S      explained the tip of the knife, having
sharp edges on either side, would show a wound with the points on either end.
Dr. S     described a deeper wound would show a point on one side and the square
on the other, and how it matched the shape of the knife blade, having the single
sharp edge on one side and a square edge on the other, below the tip. Dr. S
pointed out some of the wounds had a mark that was consistent with the
guard of the Ka Bar knife. Dr. S      described how the wounds could look
different, depending on the skin stretching, the dynamics of the situation at
the time, the angle or position the knife was in when it was inserted and or
removed, and how a victim was moving or not moving at the time.
Dr. S     described some injuries depicted that the strength of the weapon
itself, and the forced used, would have been great.

We concluded out meeting with Dr. S    . I brought item #733 back to MPD and
booked it back into MPD evidence on June 28, 2023.
