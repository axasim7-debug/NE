Concatenation of Videos

Case: 22-M09903
Officer: Det. Fox #185
Date: 7/5/2023

surveillance videos. This included 1,464 (5) minute videos that ranged from 0055
hours on 11/13/22 to 1722 hours on 11/17/2022. Using Axon Investigate version
3.1.0 software I was able to concatenate (stitch) the videos together to

placed on a portable hard drive and entered into evidence.
