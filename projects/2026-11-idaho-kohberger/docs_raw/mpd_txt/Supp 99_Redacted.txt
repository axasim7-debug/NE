BlueRidge Warrant Service

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 1/5/2023

On December 12, 2022, I applied for and was granted a warrant for sales records
from Blue Ridge Knives for Ka-Bar KA1217S, USMC Fighting Knife Sheath and known
purchases and quantities of Blue Ridge Knives from Ka-Bar including on or about
03/08/2022 the purchase of 360 units and on or about 06/24/2022 the purchase of
480 units. Blue Ridge Knives are a wholesaler for Ka-Bar knives and sheaths. FBI
SA Douglass assisted in the service of the warrant to Elan Financial.
On December 13, 2022, at approximately 0754 hours, the warrant was served via
email to     @blueridgeknives.com.
On December 16, 2022, I received an email from SA Douglass providing the data
requested pursuant to the warrant. I downloaded the files to the forensic
computers located in the secure forensic computer lab at the Moscow Police
Department.

I completed the warrant return receipt and affidavit. These documents were sent
to the Latah County Prosecutor's Office to be returned to the court. I also
emailed a copy of the receipt to Blue Ridge (    @blueridgeknives.com), as this
was the originating email address associated with the documents.
