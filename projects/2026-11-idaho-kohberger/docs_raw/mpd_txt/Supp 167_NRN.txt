3.        Narrative

I was notified via email on Monday March 20, 2023 of a found property call
reported to LCSO wherein the RP discovered a Ka Bar brand knife in some bushes
in the city of Genesee, ID on the same day. Per the LCSO call records, the knife
was located by a child playing in some trees at the top of Ash Street near
Spruce Street. Dep. Whiting responded and booked the item into LCSO evidence.

On March 21, 2023, I responded to LCSO to look at the knife. The knife was a Ka
Bar brand knife with a blade approximately four inches in length. The knife was
in a black kydex sheath with cordage wrapped around the handle.
This knife did not appear to match the wounds inflicted on all four victims. I
collected the knife from LCSO and booked it into MPD evidence on March 21, 2023.
I also attached a photo of the knife and the LCSO call record to this case file
electronically.
