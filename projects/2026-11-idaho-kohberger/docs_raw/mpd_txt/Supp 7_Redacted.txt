Lawrence - Supplemental report - Initial response

On 11-13-2022, at about 1200 hours, Capt. Berrett called me and informed me
there had been multiple victims of a homicide located in a residence on King
Road and requested all available detectives. I called Det. Vargas and was
informed she was out of town and would be back in Moscow on Monday morning. I
called Det. Mowery and he informed me he would be on his way shortly. I called
ISP Lt. Mike Mooney and told him I need assistance from ISP detectives in
processing the crime scene. Lt. Mooney put me in contact with Sgt. Gilbertson.
I briefed Sgt. Gilbertson on what I knew and he told me he would start to
assemble his team.
I responded to the scene where I met with Sgt. Gunderson. Sgt. Gunderson
informed me that officers were dispatched to an unconscious person at 1122 King
Road and that is when they found four deceased college aged people in the
residence. I told Sgt. Gunderson I needed someone from his team to return to the
Police Department to start writing an affidavit for a search warrant. Sgt.
Gunderson asked Officer Nunes to handle that task.
I then did a very brief walk through of the residence. I entered the residence
from the entrance on the North side of the building. Officer Warner who was
securing the scene logged my entrance into the residence. I then went up the
stairs and located a deceased female on the floor and male in the bed in the
West bedroom on the second floor. I climbed the stairs to the third floor and
located 2 deceased females in the south east bedroom. They were both in the
bed. I spoke with Officer Smith who was securing the residence at the south
entrance for a moment then I exited the residence through the north entrance.
Officer Warner noted my departure.

I called Sgt. Gilbertson and informed him of the additional information provided
to me and what I had seen. I told him MPD would be needing their assistance.
I also told Sgt. Gilbertson MPD had an officer writing an affidavit for a search
warrant.
I returned to the Police Department where I met with Sgt. Blaker and Cpl. Payne
whom I had called earlier and requested they respond to the PD. At this time
Det. Mowery was at the Police Department. I requested they interview the other
occupants of the residence where the deceased were located. There were also
several friends of the deceased who had been with the deceased the night before
and may have had information of their whereabouts and who they were with prior
to the murders. Those interviews started at about 1348 hours. Those to be
interviewed arrived at the PD about 1330 hours. I put them in the training room
and provided them with drinks and snacks.
Capt. Lanier arrived and asked me how he could help. I requested
he find officers that were not already tasked and complete a canvas of the
neighborhood. Capt. Lanier took care of the neighborhood canvassing.
