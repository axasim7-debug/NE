3. Narrative

On 11/13/22 at about 1200 hours I responded to 1122 King Rd for the report of a
unresponsive subject. Upon my arrival I noticed there were several college aged
subjects out front of the address. I was directed to use police caution tape to
cordon off the crime scene. As I was working around the residence I noticed the
back door was open and there were footprints in the snow coming from the back
door on the back porch. Ofc. Smith took photos of these prints with a measuring
tape. He watched the back yard area to make sure no one else came in to the
area. I noticed there were a few foot prints that left toward the west of the
back yard. This portion of the back yard is connected to two other residences,
1118 King Rd and 1126 King Rd, both directly to the west. The foot prints appear
to lead west toward King Rd. between the residences, but I was unable to see
where the foot prints went after the yards connected due to the amount of rock
landscaping. I also was unable to discern how old these prints were. There had
not been recent snow fall.

With Sgt. Gunderson, I entered the residence to continue taking photos of the
scene. I entered through the bottom floor main door, on the north side. I took
photographs of the rooms and living areas of the residence. The photos were
downloaded onto a CD and booked into evidence. The photos were labeled in this
report with a brief description of each photo.

As I was down the road of the address near the King Rd and Queen Rd
intersection, I was approached by a college aged male who identified himself as
