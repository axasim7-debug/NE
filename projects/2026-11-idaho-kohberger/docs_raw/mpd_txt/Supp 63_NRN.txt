Removing personal items from 1122 King Rd;     Capt. Berrett

On 12/07/22 at about 0945 hours, I met with MPD Command Staff members at 1122
King Road to package and remove personal items of the victims. Capt. Dahlinger
and I packaged the belongings in Kaylee Goncalves' room. I worked along the
north and west sides of the room, Capt. Dahlinger worked along the south and
east sides.

While I was packaging items, I noticed $125 dollars in cash on the dresser; 1-
$100 bill, 1 - $10 bill, 2 - $5 bill, and 5 - $1 bills. Capt. Dahlinger and I
both counted the money, and I put the it in a small clutch. I placed the clutch
in one of the boxes and marked the box that it had cash in it. Capt. Dahlinger
and I both intitialed the box.

After all the personal items that were recoverable were packaged, we moved them
to a U-Haul van and transported them to the Moscow City shop. At the shop we
unloaded the packages in a secure building.
