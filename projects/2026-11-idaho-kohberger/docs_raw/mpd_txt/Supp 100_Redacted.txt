Extreme Network Warrant

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 1/5/2023

On December 12, 2022, I applied for and was granted a warrant for Extreme
Networks to assist in unlocking a laptop located in the residence located at
1122 King Road. The laptop was being used by Kaylee Goncalves as a paid employee
of Extreme Networks. The warrant requested permission for Extreme Networks to
unlock or provide a password for the laptop.

Sgt Neil Uhrig assisted in the forensic examination of the laptop. I forwarded
the warrant to Sgt Uhrig who then served the warrant on December 12, 2022, at
approximately 1519 hours via email to                @hupd.harvard.edu.
On December 14, 2022, Sgt Uhrig received an email providing the encryption key
for the laptop. This allowed the data from the laptop to be forensically
acquired and examined by Sgt Uhrig.
I completed the warrant return receipt and affidavit. These documents were sent
to the Latah County Prosecutor's Office to be returned to the court. A copy of
the receipt was emailed to Extreme Networks (               @hupd.harvard.edu),
as this was the originating email address associated with the warrant.
