Supplemental Report - Packing personal items at residence.

On December 7, 2022 at approximately 0945 hours I responded to 1122 King Road
with other members of the command team to pack personal items of the residents
who lived there. I worked with Det. Vargas and Admin Assistant K     P      to
pack B         room which was on the first floor, east end. I primarily built
boxes and packed up all of the clothes and shoes in the closet as well as a few
bedding items.
The next room we worked on was a bed room not in use on the first floor, west
end. In that room we packed a few misc. items, a computer, shoes, golf clubs and
various items that were stored in the room and the owner was uncertain.
The last room we packed D       room on the second floor off of the kitchen at
the bottom of the stairs. My role in that room was primarily taping boxes
together then taping boxes shut and making them with D       name or the initial
"D".
After completing the packing protion Chief Fry arrived with a U Haul van. I
stayed in the back of the van and packed boxes as they were carried out from the
residence by other team members. After the van was packed, I followed the van,
driven by Chief Fry, in my patrol car to the city shop secure storage building.
At that point all of the team members unloaded boxes and stacked them by room in
the storage building the secured the door and left.
