On 11-13-22, at approximately 1158 hours, I responded to a report of an
unconscious person at 1122 King Rd. Dispatch advised a female was passed out
from intoxication and not waking up. Dispatch advised there was a male seen in
the room with the female last night. A short time later, dispatch advised the
female was not conscious and not breathing.
As I arrived, Ofc. Nunes advised over the air it appeared to be a homicide. I
saw a small group of females outside the home in the parking lot. One female
advised their roommate was on the second floor and needed help. I went to Ofc.
Nunes' location at the west bedroom on the second floor and passed a male in the
living room later identified as H      J      . Ofc. Nunes advised the female
and a male in the bedroom had blood on them and appeared to be beyond help. I
walked out of the bedroom toward J       and saw a clean steak knife on a beer
pong table. I pointed out the knife to Nunes. J         said he got the steak
knife from the kitchen because he arrived and saw the female on the bedroom
floor covered in blood and did not know if there was anyone else present in the
room. J        said he checked the room with the knife for protection.
Ofc. Nunes and I walked to the first floor entrance with H     . I asked the
females and H      if there was anyone else in the apartment and they did not
know. I told Ofc. Nunes the home was not secure and we started the process of
clearing it.
