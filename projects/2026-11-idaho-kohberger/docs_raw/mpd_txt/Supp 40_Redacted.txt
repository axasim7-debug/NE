Router Interrogation

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 11/15/2022

On November 13, 2022, I responded to 1122 King Road in Moscow, Idaho in an
attempt to interrogate the wireless router located at this residence. After
speaking with Cpl Brett Payne, he informed me the wireless router and cable
modem were located in the living room on the second floor.
After arrival, I used an ethernet cable to connect my laptop to the Local Area
Network (LAN) ports on the back of the Spectrum WiFi 6 router with the Serial #
               . The router then provided my laptop with an IP address and
Gateway IP, which resolved to the router.
I attempted to locate a web-based management portal for the device but was
prompted to download the Spectrum app and use the account username and password
to access the device. I contacted Spectrum technical support and found this was
the only way for the client to access the data on the device.

I then called the Charter Law Enforcement Response Team (LERT) and spoke with a
representative and tried to ascertain if Charter/Spectrum could retrieve data
from the router via their diagnostic portal. After several days and phone calls
to Charter, I spoke with T     D      , the manager of the Legal Response
Operations Center. D       stated her team was working on verifying if the
router hardware was able to be accessed.
On November 17, 2022, I again called Charter and left a voicemail. D
returned my phone call and stated her technicians were able to access and
download the data from the router, however, the did not preserve the correct
timeframe specified in my request. I was told she would have her technicians
retry and preserve the data.

received a call back from D       who stated the technicians were able to
