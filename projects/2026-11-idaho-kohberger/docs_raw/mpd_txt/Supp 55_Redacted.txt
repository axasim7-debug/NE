Department of Labor Warrant

Case# 22-M09903
Date: 12/5/2022

On December 4, 2022, I found I had received an email forwarded to me from K
W      of the Idaho Department of Labor. The email was from Michael Douglass of
the Federal Bureau of Investigation. SA Douglass assisted in the service of the
warrant to the Department of Labor.
The email contained a single file with the data requested pursuant to the
warrant served on the IDoL. The file was downloaded and saved onto a computer
located in the secure computer forensic lab located at the Moscow Police
Department.
In examining the file, it showed the requested parties name, DOB and SSN with a
corresponding screen shot of their reported income.
I completed the warrant return receipt and affidavit. These documents were sent
to the Latah County Prosecutor's Office to be returned to the court. I also
emailed a copy of the receipt to W      (            @labor.idaho.gov), as this
was the originating email address associated with the documents.
