On Wednesday, September 13, 2023 FBI Tech Specialist T        forwarded me tip
serial #9237 via email. The tip was called in on August 22, 2023 to the FBI tip
line. The caller, R       T    , advised the call taker he saw a person whom he
thought was Kohberger at 1122 King Road on two occasions in July on 2022. T
advised he came to 1122 King Road as he was a real estate agent looking for
property for clients. T     stated on both occasions he saw Kohberger and
Kohberger's father.
On Wednesday, September 13, 2023 I called T    on the telephone after verifying
his identity via TLO. T    was a licensed real estate agent living in Florida. I
asked him to relate to me what he reported to the FBI. T    told me he drove his
nephew, "T    ", to the western US from Florida as his nephew was dying from
cancer. T     advised he came through Moscow and saw a sign for property for
sale, so he followed the sign to 1122 King Road to look at the property for
clients. While he was parked out front, T    said a brown "Jeep type pickup"
pulled into the parking lot driven by an older man with a red beard. T    said
he then saw a male who he was sure was Kohberger get up from the back seat. T
and the driver exchanged small talk, and T    said he walked to the back door to
speak to the tenants but no one answered. T    said he left the area with his
nephew shortly after that.

T    said he returned to 1122 King Road on his way back to Florida. T    said he
was parked in front of the house when a white car, which he described as "the
same one they've shown on television", drive by the house. T    said he was sure
the driver was Kohberger's father and Kohberger was in the passenger seat. T
said the vehicle sped away and he did not see where it went.

homicides occurred. I told him it was November to which he replied it must have
been around then. T     previously told the FBI he was in Moscow in July. T
then told me his nephew passed away a short time after he returned from his
trip. I asked T     when his nephew passed away. T    told me his nephew passed
away eight years ago. I thanked T     for his time and told him I would call him
with further questions if needed.
At this time, there is nothing to corroborate T      statements based off
