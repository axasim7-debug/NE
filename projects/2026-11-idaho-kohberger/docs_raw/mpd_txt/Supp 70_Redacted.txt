Walmart Warrant Service

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 12/8/2022

On November 21, 2022, I applied for, and was granted, a warrant to Wal-Mart for
related sales of the following items for the period of January 1, 2022 to
present:
1. Ka-Bar KA1217S, USMC Fighting Knife Sheath;
2. Ka-Bar Full Size USMC Straight Edge Knife;

On November 28, 2022, at approximately 1316 hours, the warrant was served via
the Law Enforcement Portal (     @walmartlegal.com). Forensic Account M
D        with the Federal Bureau of Investigations assisted in the production
and service of the warrants.
On December 8, 2022, I received a packet from Walmart containing paper documents
and two (2) DVDs with sales data on them from the local Walmart in Moscow,
Idaho. These files were downloaded to the forensic computers located in the
secure forensic computer lab at the Moscow Police Department.
On December 9, 2022, I received notice from FA D        the data requested
pursuant to the warrant was received. The data files were downloaded to the
forensic computers located in the secure forensic computer lab at the Moscow
Police Department.

A warrant return receipt and affidavit was completed. These documents were sent
to the Latah County Prosecutor's Office to be returned to the court. I also
emailed a copy of the receipt to Tinder       @walmartlegal.com), as this was
the originating email address associated with the documents.
