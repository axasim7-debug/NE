3.        Narrative

On Thursday, June 8, 2023 I accessed Kohberger's white Hyundai Elantra in order
to photograph items in the trunk compartment. The items were identified as being
ordered via Amazon by the same account Kohberger used to purchase the Ka Bar
knife with sheath.
I was able to identify three items from the trunk compartment which I could
positively identify as the same items ordered from Amazon per the return
obtained via subpoena in this case. The first item was a bottle of supplements
named "Amandean Vitamin D3 K2. Vegan" on the Amazon return. The bottle was in
the trunk compartment and was contained in a small plastic bag. The bottle's
label read "Amandean Vegan D3+K2 Dietary Supplements".

The second item I positively identified was labeled as "LEADBRAND Scissor Jack
1.5 Tons" on the Amazon return. I found a 1.5 ton scissor jack in the trunk
compartment but it did not have any brand markings. I searched the same name on
Amazon and found the same item I located in the trunk compartment. It should
be noted the jack came with a ratchet handle as well. This item was booked
previously.
The third item I located was labeled "CARTMAN Warning Triangle DOT Approved" on
the Amazon return. I located a red, rectangular plastic box with warning
triangles inside. On the side of the box was a sticker which read "cartman" on
the top of the sticker and "Cartman Warning Triangle DOT Approved (3pk) New" on
the bottom of the sticker.
