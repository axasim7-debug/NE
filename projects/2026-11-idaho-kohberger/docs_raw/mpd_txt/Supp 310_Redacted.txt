Vitamin Supplement seized from Kohberger Vehicle:

On April 3, 2025, I assisted T   S          , and the Prosecution team, with
reviewing the evidence in this case. A question was brought up if a Amandean
Vegan D3+K2 vitamin supplement bottle, which had been seen in several
photographs, had been booked into evidence and S           stated it had not.
S           checked with Det. Payne and was informed the vitamin bottle was
still located in the trunk of Kohberger's white Elantra, MPD ID#: 23M-0204.
I broke the evidence seal on the driver's side door of the Elantra and opened
the trunk with the release lever (also breaking the evidence seal on the trunk).
 I located the Amandean D3-K2 vitamin bottle in the trunk in a plastic zip lock
baggie. I collected the bottle out of the trunk, counted the soft gel capsules
in the bottle (22 capsules out of 120), and booked the bottle into MPD evidence.
 I placed new evidence seals on the driver's side door and trunk of the vehicle.
 I took photos of the seals, and of the bottle, and copied the photos to a DVD
and booked the DVD into MPD evidence.
Item booked: Amandean Vegan D3+K2 Dietary Supplement, 125 MCG Vitamin D3 5000
IU/100 MCG vitamin K2 as K2VITAL, 120 Vegan soft gels: 22 capsules remaining.
