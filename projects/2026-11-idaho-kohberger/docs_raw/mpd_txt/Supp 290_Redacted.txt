the man was wearing khaki pants, brown shoes, a salmon colored button up shirt,
a surgical COVID type mask, and purple surgical gloves.
J       said she didn't think anything of it at the time but thought she should
let law enforcement know after seeing the photos of Kohberger. J       did not
have further information regarding the incident. I thanked her for the
