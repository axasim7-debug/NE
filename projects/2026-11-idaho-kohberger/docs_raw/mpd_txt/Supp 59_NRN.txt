Victim Phone Extractions

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 12/4/2022

On November 15, 2022, I contacted Det David Taylor of the Coeur d'Alene Police
Department with a request for an agency assist. I requested assistance unlocking
the cell phones of Kaylee Goncalves, Maddie Mogen, Xana Kernodle and Ethan
Chapin, the victims in this case. I emailed Det Taylor a copy of the signed
warrant describing the phones as being searchable.

On November 16, 2022, the phones were transported to the CDA Police Department
for a forensic brute force attempt to unlock them, however Det Taylor stated he
was not able to extract any information from the locked phones and the phones
were not supported for the brute force pin code extraction. The phones were then
returned to the Moscow Police Department on November 17, 2022.

I then asked for assistance from Cpt Tyson Barrett in attempting to gain pin
codes from family members to access the data on the devices. I was provided pin
codes for Mogen, Kernodle and Chapin, which successfully unlocked the devices.
The pin codes provided for Goncalves' phone did not unlock the device.
On the three devices I was able to unlock, I performed advanced logical
extractions on the devices at the Moscow Police Department. A report for each
device will be completed separately.

On November 20, 2022, I again requested the assistance of Det Taylor in gaining
a full-file system extraction on the three unlocked devices. The phones were
delivered to CDA Police Department on November 21, 2022. On November 22, 2022,
Det Taylor stated he would not be able to complete the extractions.
On November 23, 2022, I went to the CDA Police Department and picked up the
phones. I then took them to the Post Falls Police Department and spoke with Det
Neil Uhrig. Det Uhrig informed me his forensic tools would not support a
full-file system extraction.
On November 25, 2022, I went to the Walla Walla Police Department and spoke with
Det Chris Ruchert. Det Ruchert stated her forensic tools would be able to
support full-file system extractions on the unlocked devices, and would be able
to complete limited extraction on Goncalves' locked phone. I left the phones
with Det Ruchert to be extracted.
On November 28, 2022, Det Ruchert informed me the phone extractions were
complete and ready to be picked up. On November 29, 2022, Det Justin Deane
picked up the phones and returned them to the Moscow Police Department. I
received the four devices and placed them in the secure computer forensic lab.
The extracted data was copied to the forensic computers and verified by hash
values.

An examination of the data will be completed at my earliest convenience.
