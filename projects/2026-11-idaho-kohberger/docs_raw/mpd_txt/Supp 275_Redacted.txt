On Thursday, April 25, 2024, I received a note from MPD reception to call C
T          /1943) in reference to this case. I called T     who agreed to come to
MPD to speak to me. I spoke to T    in interview room #1. This is a summary of
my conversation with T    and not a verbatim transcription.

I asked T    what made her decide to speak to me on this date. T    told me her
good friends had convinced her to come talk to MPD to make sure what she knew
was at least conveyed to law enforcement. I asked T    to tell me what she knew.
T    told me she and her husband went to the Costco in Clarkston, WA on 11/13/22
to pick up new glasses for T   . She told me they left Costco around 1400-1430
hours that afternoon and drove north across Red Wolf Bridge. T    told me when
they turned Eastbound on the highway from the bridge she saw a man walking in
the grassy area between the river and the highway.

T    stated she thought this was strange as there was nothing for anyone to be
doing there. T    stated the man was walking eastbound and noticed her staring
at him. T    said the man looked up at her an then appeared to look down and
avoid her gaze. I asked T    what the man looked like. T    told me she had seen
much of the news coverage regarding Kohberger and that was what came to her
mind, but that she was not certain he was the person she saw. I asked her if the
man she saw would match Kohberger's appearance and she said it would. I asked
T    what the man was wearing. She told me she thought it was strange as the man
was wearing nice clothes. I asked if business casual was a fair classification
and she said it was.
T    stated she did not see a car the man was walking to and thought that was
strange. T    said they drove away and that was all she knew. T    had drawn a
map of the location. I booked the drawing into MPD evidence.

once you turn east off the bridge. Anyone walking in the area would have to be
very near the guardrail as the hill is relatively steep and drops away from the
