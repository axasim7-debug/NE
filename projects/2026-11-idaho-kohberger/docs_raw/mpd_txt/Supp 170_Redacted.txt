2.        Evidence
Item 670: Knives
3.        Narrative

I was notified by ISP Det. Roberts at approximately 1300 hours on March 21, 2023
that a land owner near Genesee, ID found two knives in a roadside ditch. Det.
Roberts was not available, so I called to speak to the reporting party, R
H     . H      stated he was cleaning out irrigation ditches when he noticed two
kitchen style knives with duct taped handles in the ditch. H      did not touch
the knives and notified ISP. H      told me where the knives were and I
responded to collect them.
The location provided was in a ditch across the road from the driveway to
Old Highway 95. I could immediately see the knives laying in the water and took
several photos of the knives prior to collection. Upon collection, I observed
one knife was a large butcher knife and the other was a small steak knife. The
handles of both knives were wrapped in duct tape. Prior to leaving, I conducted
one more visual search of the area and located a makeshift duct tape sheath in
the culvert below where the knives were located. The sheath fit both knives at
the same time. I collected the sheath as well.
Upon further examination, neither knife appeared to match the wounds on the four
victims. The larger knife blade was too wide to have inflicted the wounds on the
victims and the smaller stake knife appeared to be too short. However, I booked
both items in case further examination was needed. I booked the knives into MPD
evidence and attached photos to this case electronically.
