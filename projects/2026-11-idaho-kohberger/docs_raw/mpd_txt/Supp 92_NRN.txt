Supplemental - Lanier, Disaster Services response.

On December 30, 2022 at approximately 0845 hours, Disaster Services responded to
1122 King Rd. to being cleaning the residence of biohazards and other chemicals
used during the crime scene processing. This was a pre-arranged response. As
members of Disater Response were setting up their tents and barriers I received
a call from Latah County Prosecutor Bill Thompson. Mr. Thompson advised me that
a judges order had just been issued requesting all mitigation and clean up be
halted immediately.
At that point I instructed the Disaster Services crew members to stop. I
confirmed that the door to the residence had not been opened and no one had
entered the residence yet. I checked the evidence tape on the door to ensure the
seal had not been broken. I stood by while all of the equipment was loaded back
into the response trailer and the entire team left the area. I returned the
scene back over to the Northern States Security guard and replaced the barricade
tape across the parking area entrance. I left the scene at that time.
