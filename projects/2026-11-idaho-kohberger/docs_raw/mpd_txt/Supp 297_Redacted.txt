3.        Narrative
On Tuesday, August 6, 2024 I received another discovery request from the defense
counsel in this case asking for documentation about a tip received, and later
acknowledged in an MPD press release, about a white Hyundai Elantra that was
found in Eugene, OR. The FBI tip line also received numerous tips about this
vehicle. It was found that no report was written at the time which documented
what information was obtained by the investigative team. However, the vehicle
was deemed to be irrelevant to the investigation.
In order to obtain the information likely used to make this determination, I
contacted Eugene PD on August 8, 2024 and requested any information they had
about the white Elantra. I later received a secure email from EPD containing
four attachments. The first was a press release reference the vehicle. The other
three were incident reports about the vehicle. The following information was
obtained from the incident reports:
The white Elantra, bearing Colorado license plate         was involved in a
crash on 12/17/22 in Eugene, OR. Law enforcement and fire responded and
contacted the driver. The vehicle was reported two other times (hence the other
two reports). One driver's license was run during the report and was listed as
A        R   . The vehicle was crashed and had extensive front end damage.
The RO of the vehicle was determined to be A    G         . It is unclear who
contacted G         at the time of the report, but the determination was made
that the vehicle was of no interest to investigators.
The information provided by EPD is attached to this case.
