Additional Video Downloads

Case# 22-M09903
Date: 10/25/2023

On October 17, 2023, I received an email from the Latah County Prosecutor's
Office in reference to downloading additional video from the security camera
seized from the residence located at      King Road in Moscow, Idaho.
Previously video had been downloaded from the camera for the approximately time
frame of 0230 to 0430 hours on November 13, 2022. These videos were downloaded
and contained audio. Through different methods using forensically acquired data
from the camera's SDcard, additional videos were downloaded, however, they did
not include the audio.
I was requested to download videos, to include audio, from the approximate time
frames of 0445 to 1200 hours on November 13, 2022. Due to the fact the camera
light bulb connection was inadvertently broken previously, a repair to the
device to allow it to be powered on was needed. In the email received on October
17, 2023, Defense Counsel Anne Taylor provided approval of the repair. Prior
approval was provided by the Latah County Prosecutor's Office.
I was able to request and receive the camera and wireless router from evidence.
I was successful in replacing the light-bulb threaded connection with a standard
wall-plug to power the device. The device powered up and I was able to download
videos from 0445 hours to 1205 hours from the device. Upon reviewing the videos,
they appeared to contain audio.
The videos were saved to a forensic computer located in the secure Moscow Police
Department. These videos were concatenated using Axon Investigate (formerly
called Input-Ace) version# 3.2.0. These files were placed onto Blu-Ray disks and
placed into evidence. I returned the camera and router to evidence after
resealing their respective containers.
