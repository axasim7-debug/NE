Initial response;   Capt. Berrett

On 11/13/22 at around 1200 hours, I received a phone call from Sgt. Gunderson,
informing me his team responded to a call at 1122 King Road where they found
four, deceased, college age individuals inside the residence. He said it was
apparent they had all been murdered. Sgt. Gunderson said they did not know the
location or identity of the suspect.

Sgt. Gunderson said his team had cleared the residence and secured the
scene. He said they identified a group of college age individuals outside the
residence and had started interviewing them.
I called Chief Fry, Capt. Lanier and Capt. Dahlinger to let them know about the
homicide. I called Det. Sgt. Lawrence, told him about the homicide and asked
him to call in detectives. Sgt. Lawrence said he would call in Sgt. Blaker and
Cpl. Payne to assist as well, because they were slated to rotate into detectives
soon. Sgt. Lawrence said he was also calling the Idaho State Police to ask the
Region 2 detectives to process the crime scene.
I called B      E     , U of I Dean of Students, and B     L      with U of I
Office of Public Safety and Security to inform them of the homicide because the
residence was near the U of I campus and the victims appeared to be college
students.
When I arrived on scene, I met with Sgt. Gunderson and Sgt. Lawrence on the
street in front of the residence. We briefly discussed the scene and I was given
the names of the four individuals who were killed; Ethan Chapin, Xana Kernodel,
Madison Mogen and Kaylee Goncalves. I was also told two of the witnesses
outside, D     M         and B       F    , were roommates and were apparently
inside the residence when the incident occurred.
Sgt. Gunderson told me a dog was taken out of Kaylee Goncalves' room and
transported to the humane society for safe keeping. He told me it didn't appear
the dog had gone into the rooms where the murders were committed because it
didn't have any visible blood on its body.
I contacted U of I Dean E     , gave him the victims names and asked if he could
verify if they were students. He contacted me later and confirmed all four were
students, but didn't have any emergency contact information for them other than

have a patrol officer meet D       at the animal shelter to release the dog.
I continued to work with detectives and command staff for the rest of the day.
