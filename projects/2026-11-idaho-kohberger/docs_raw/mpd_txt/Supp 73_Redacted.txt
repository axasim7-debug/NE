3.        Narrative
On 11/23/22 I applied for and was granted a search warrant for     Queen Road,
   , the residence of J    S        . The warrant was based off the following
information:

OFC House and I responded to     Queen Road at approximately 1000 hours on
11/15/22. We walked on the common walkway in front of the door to S
apartment and observed the exterior of the door. The area around the door had
numerous spots of a dried fluid which appeared to be rust drops from a metal
roof above the apartment's door. These drops were mainly on the wall next to the
door frame, not on the frame itself. I observed what appeared to be a different
fluid spot on the door approximately eight inches up from the bottom of the door
under the door knob. I also noticed the dead bolt plating was clean, polished
silver and had no dirt on it. I walked away from the door in order to conduct an
interview on a possible witness. OFC House and several members of the ISP
forensics unit to include Detective Hugh Powell, walked back to     Queen Road,
   .
I met back up with OFC House and Det. Powell a short time later and they told me
they believed the spot of dried fluid on the door displayed similar
characteristics to that of dried blood. Namely, Det. Powell stated the spot
displayed a cracking texture common when blood dries on a surface. The spot also
displayed a similar drop pattern to the blood spots found on the banister wall
and the wall on the stairwell to the ground floor which I observed inside 1122
entryway into the living room. Samples of each were collected 94 in total) and
ISP Det. Talbott took photos of the residence. Upon completion, I left the
requried paperwork on the couch in the living room and Det. Talbott took a photo

The search warrant was returned on 12/4/22.
