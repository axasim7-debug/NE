3.        Narrative

Based off information obtained during the course of this investigation, I
applied for an arrest warrant for Bryan C. Kohberger. Magistrate Judge Marshall
signed the warrant on 12/29/22.
Previous search warrants and information led investigators to believe Kohberger
left Pullman, WA and returned to his parents home in Allbrightsville, PA. Sgt.
Gilberston (ISP), T      S       (FBI) and I flew to Pennsylvania on 12/29/22
and met with the Pennsylvania State Police(PSP) and FBI personnel there.

At approximately 0100 hours on 12/30/22, the PSP SERT team conducted an
operation at                   and took Kohberger into custody based off the
warrant signed by Judge Marshall.
Kohberger was taken to a nearby PSP barracks where Sgt. Gilbertson and I
interviewed him for approximately 45 minutes. See Sgt. Gilbertson's report on
the interview for full details.
The warrant was served to Kohberger upon his extradition back to Idaho and his
booking into the Latah County Jail.
