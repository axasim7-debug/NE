3.      Narrative

On November 12, 2022, at about 2100 hours, I began an overtime shift for a plain
clothes detail on the University of Idaho campus and related areas. This patrol
was for minors in possession/consumption of alcohol along with other
alcohol/drug offenses. During this patrol I and the other officer with me, Ofc.
Rosendahl, only had our body worn cameras. We were operating out of the Moscow
Police Department Ford Fusion which I drove throughout the entire detail. This
particular department vehicle did not have a dash camera or any other recording
device. My street time conducting the plain clothes detail concluded at 0352
hours on November 13, 2022.
During this detail, Ofc. Rosendahl and I made several patrols through the area
of King Street and Queen Road. During each patrol of the area we travelled on
the north side of 500 Queen Road, heading east. We wrapped around the building
heading south on the east side, then west on the south side of the complex. We
then travelled north on the west side, turned west on to Queen Road and
proceeded to King Street.
During one of the patrols in this areas, while approaching the southwest corner
of 500 Queen Road, I recall seeing a darker colored vehicle with taillights on.
This vehicle was parked facing south, on the south side of the parking lot,
towards the west end. There was a short section of the parking lot which
extended to the west of 500 Queen Road, south of 1122 King Street, which was
where the vehicle was.

Each time we patrolled this area, I drove the same route. During the final
patrol, which to the best of my recollection was between 0200 and 0300 hours on
November 13, 2022, as we travelled north on the west side of the apartment
complex, I saw several individuals approaching the residence at 1122 King
Street. During this detail, I was looking for alcoholic beverages, open
alcoholic containers, and individuals who appeared to be intoxicated. I observed
the individuals as I drove pass but did not see any reason to contact them for
our related patrol. I recall my view of the individuals being obstructed by
vehicles and brush on the east side of the parking lot for 1122 King Street. The
individuals were walking close a vehicle which was parked in the northeast
corner of the lot. As I turned onto Queen Road and travelled west, I continued
to view the individuals. From my recollection, there were at least two
individuals, possibly three. I recall one of the individuals having blonde
hair. I do not recall exactly how many individuals there were, what they were
wearing, or any other details as I quickly looked for beverages in their hands
and whether they were staggering from intoxication.
Since we made several patrols through the area, I cannot say with 100%
confidence I saw the vehicle on the same pass as I saw the individuals.
Upon review of my radio history, I contacted three individuals at     Taylor
Avenue at about 0255 hours. To the best of my recollection, this was after
exiting King Street after seeing the individuals at 1122 King Street. We cleared
this contact at 0332 hours.
