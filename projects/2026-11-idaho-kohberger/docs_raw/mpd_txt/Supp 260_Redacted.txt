On Friday, March 8, 2024, I received an email from PA Thompson reference the
receipt of a phone survey. The recipient, N        B     , stated someone called
her and began to ask questions about the Kohberger trial to include questions
about how B      felt about the case. See below for B        information
provided via emailed Word document:

"Phone number #1             , lasted 18 minutes.
Stated they were a company performing a survey among the community in relation
to the Brian Kohberger case. They verified if I was a Latah County resident and
which city I considered myself a resident.
Here is a small list of the questions that I can remember. These questions are
not verbatim but of the general sense of what I can remember being asked.
1.      Have I read, listened to, or seen any news reels regarding Brian
Kohberger and/or the families of the victims?
2.      Have I read, listened to, or seen any television or movie documentaries
regarding Brian Kohberger and/or the families of the victims?
3.      Were you aware of any television, movies or documentaries concerning
Brian Kohberger on sources like Netflix?
4.      What sources have you listened to, read or seen as a source of
information?
5.      Can you name the specific newspapers, news stations, or source of
information that you received your knowledge of the case?

6.      Have I heard or participated in any rumors surrounding Brian Kohberger,
the victims or their families? (I responded "no" but that I've heard rumors
regarding law enforcement involved in the case).
7.      In a general statement, how would you describe how   the community has
been affected by the murders?
8.     Has the murders caused anyone you know or yourself any excess stress?

9.      Do you feel the community has felt an excess of stress since the
murders?

10.    Do you know a student that attends University of Idaho?
11.     Do you know or are related to law enforcement   and/or anyone directly
involved with the case?
12.     In a general statement, do you feel   Brian Kohberger could receive a
fair trial?
13.     Do you think if you were asked to serve on a jury would you be able to
serve fairly weighing the evidence and the case presented?
14.     Have you ever driven past the residence in which the murders were
committed?
15.     Did you ever read, watch or listen to any reports of Brian Kohberger's
arrest in Pennsylvania?
16.     How would you describe how closely you've been following the Brian
Kohberger case?
I can't remember much more but I felt the questions were sometimes redundant
just changing some words here and there and they were focused on my personal
opinion and my opinion of the community surrounding BOTH the victims, their
families and Brian Kohberger. There were several questions regarding if I felt
Kohberger could get a fair trial and if I felt I could impartially or fairly sit
on a jury. The questions were just worded differently but that was the general
sense they were trying to feel out from me in my responses."

On Monday, March 11, 2024 I received another report from PA Thompson stating
J     M      received a similar phone call. I called Mrs. M      and learned the
following. The business that called M      went by the name Research Strategies
Inc.. The company's website, https://researchstrategiesinc.com, states they
conduct various types of surveys to include public opinion surveys and
post-trial jury analysis. The phone number provided to Ms. M      shows up on
the Better Business Bureau website for the same company out of Alabama. I
checked TLO and found the number used to be associated with a woman in Alabama
who passed away 2011 which further lends to it being the correct number, just
reassigned to this business.
I spoke to Mrs. M      who stated she received the call appx. 4:51 pm on
3/10/24. The first question she was asked was if she was a Latah County
resident. There were several questions that followed which asked generic
questions about trials and the criminal justice system. Then she stated the
woman asked her specifically if she was aware of the Kohberger trial. Mrs.
M      stated she refused to answer, asked the woman who she worked for
(Research Strategies, with the number provided), who was paying for the survey,
and asked for her supervisor. The woman gave her the company's number and stated
she was unaware of who was responsible for the survey. Mrs. M      also stated
the number the woman called from was             . This number is not found in
TLO and is likely a Google voice type number not associated with a person. This
is also the number that called B     .
Mrs. M      stated she posted to her Facebook page asking if anyone else had
received similar calls. She stated several people in the Potlatch area had
received calls from the same number that called her but most were not answered.
I asker her to keep me posted if she became aware of any further information.

My conclusions are this is a legitimate survey requested via this company. Open
source searches suggest a study like this would cost into the tens of thousands
of dollars. I called the number provide for Research Strategies Inc. and was
directed to leave a message. The recording also stated I had reached Research
Strategies Inc.
