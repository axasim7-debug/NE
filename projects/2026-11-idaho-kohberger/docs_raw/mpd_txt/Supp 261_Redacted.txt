3.     Narrative

On 03/12/24, I received an email from Detective Cpl. Payne asking for a review
and detail of my BWC regarding this case and my involvement. Due to my minimal
involvement and lack of discovered information, I had not completed a supplement
at the time.
On 11/13/22, at approximately 1420 hours, I responded to 1122 King Rd to assist
with canvassing the immediate area and I was tasked with conducting door to door
contact in the     Queen Rd. area. I was informed that a likely time of the
incident was between approximately 0400 and 0445. I was advised that other
officers had already covered the south facing apartments of Queen Rd and I began
going door to door on the center and north facing portions of the apartment
complex with Cpt. Lanier. I spoke with several tenants who stated they did not
hear anything strange and their only attention to 1122 King Rd. was brought upon
by the increased police presence through the day. I did not identify the tenants
I spoke to who stated they had not been awake or alerted to any unusual sounds,
people or events. Of the tenants I spoke with and did not identify, all gave a
similar account of standard party activity and nothing out of the ordinary for a
typical Saturday night/Sunday morning.
While Cpt. Lanier and I were conducting door to door canvassing, our attention
was drawn to dark red colored drops and splotches on several portions of the
walkway of the building (particularly on the upper level near apt     and after
further inspection, I could see that this same colored substance appeared to be
rust which ran down the walls and was on several door jams as well as the siding
throughout the apartment complex. I checked the walkway back from the area and
did not see any other drops or splotches indicating a blood trail leading toward
1122 King Rd. There was no answer at apartment     Based on those observations,
I determined the drops and splotches to be uninvolved or unrelated.
I spoke with the tenants in apartment    who stated they went to bed around 0100
hours and music was present at 1122 King Rd. during that time, which they stated
was "pretty much every weekend". Both tenants said they woke up around 0930
hours and there were no strange or alarming sounds. Due to having no information
aside from standard weekend behavior from 1122 King Rd, I did not identify the
two contacts.
I spoke with the tenants in apartment     identified as J        G
(     /2000) and I       B          /03). The two stated they were at a birthday
party on Blake St. (approximately 1.5 blocks away) and got to bed in the early
morning hours, estimated to be approximately 0400 hours. I asked which way they
walked back from the party and B    described them as walking back, west on
Taylor Ave, then cutting up a stair path just west of     Taylor Ave. (the
stairs travel from the parking lot of     Taylor Ave. up to Queen Rd, facing
straight at the gravel parking lot of 1122 King Rd.). Both B    and G
stated they did not see anyone outside or pass anyone as they were walking. I
asked if there was still a party or music going on at 1122 King Rd. when they
walked by and they stated there was. Nothing seemed out of the ordinary and they
did not hear anything that alarmed them in any way.

After conducting the canvas through my assigned area, I cleared the scene. At no
time during my involvement in the area did I cross into the designated crime
