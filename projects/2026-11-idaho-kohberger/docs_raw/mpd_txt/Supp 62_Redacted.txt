3.        Narrative

On December 7, 2022 at approximately 1000 hours, I was assisting with the
packing and moving of personal belongings out of the residence at 1122 King St.
Capt. Berrett and I were in the process of packing items from Kaylee Goncalves'
room on the third floor. Inside the closet I was removing items from a black
plastic three drawer oraganizer. In the bottom drawer I located a Kodak
Powerflash disposable camera and a GoPro camera in a small black zippered pouch.
I was unaware if these items had been located during any of the previous
searches of the residence so I took custody of them until I could learn that
information. I advised Capt. Berrett of the items and showed them to him. After
completing the packing and moving of personal belongings I brought the GoPro and
the Kodak camera back to the police department. Prior to leaving the residence I
also took possession of the keys to all the vehicles belonging to the deceased.
Once back at the police deparment I locked the items into a temporary storage
locker in the evidence processing room.

I later contacted the investigative team and learned the memory card from the
GoPro was seized during a previous search of the residence so the camera itself
was of no evidentiary value. I was told the Kodak disposable camera should be
booked into evidence.
On December 8, 2022 I met with Property Coordinator T   S          . S
assisted me in booking the Kodak camera into evidence. I then took the vehicle
keys and the GoPro camera to the City Shop where the rest of the personal
belongings from the 1122 King St. residence were being stored. I placed the
GoPro camera into one of the boxes containing Kaylee Goncalves' belongings. I
left the vehicles keys (contained in a ziplock bag) also in the storage area
with the other belongings.
