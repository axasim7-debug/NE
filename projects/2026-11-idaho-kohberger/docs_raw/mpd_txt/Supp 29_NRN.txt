3.        Narrative

On November 19, 2022 at approximately 1200 hours, I met with Det. Mowery who
tasked me with downloading surveillance video footage from several usb memory
drives. Det. Mowery gave me multiple small manila envelopes which contained usb
memory drives collected from multiple different investigators. Det. Mowery asked
me to download the videos from all the drives and to place them on an external
harddrive which he provided to me. Once that task was complete, Det. Mowery
asked me to copy all of the videos from the portable harddrive to the M:/ drive
on our City of Moscow network where he created a folder were working copies of
the videos could be stored.
After downloading the videos from the individual usb memory drives, I placed
them back into the manila envelopes they came from and sealed them with evidence
tape. I did find two of the usb memory drives, which came from the same
envelope, did not contain any video footage and I returned them to Det. Mowery.
Once all the videos were downloaded to the portable harddrive, I then copied
them all to the M:/ drive on the City network and returned the harddrive to Det.
Mowery.
