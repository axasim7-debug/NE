3.        Narrative
At approximately 1600 hours on 11/13/22, SGT Blaker and I responded to 1122 King
Road to assist with scene security processing. Upon our arrival, the ISP
Forensic Team was on scene and was preparing to begin processing the scene. OFC
Smith, one of the initial responding officers to the incident, advised he would
walk me through the scene.
OFC Smith and I entered 1122 King Road through the bottom floor door on the
north side of the building. Upon entering, I observed a small bedroom on the
west side of the bottom floor. The door was ajar and I could see a bed with
covers and several other miscellaneous personal items strewn about. The room did
not appear to have been occupied by anyone as I did not see any clothes inside.
I followed OFC Smith down the hall where there was a small bathroom. Next to the
small bathroom to the east was what I later learned was a utility closet. This
closet had a pin code style lock and was secured. I looked into the bedroom on
the east end of the bottom floor, which I later learned belonged to B
F    . The room had normal clothes and personal items about but otherwise did
not seem out of the ordinary.
OFC Smith and I then walked upstairs to the second floor. The stairs ended in a
small living room. There was a sectional style couch along the east and south
wall of the room. There was also a small TV in a void on the northeast corner of
the room. I also saw a black backpack on the floor just east of the top of the
stairs. OFC Smith then directed me down the hallway to the west bedroom on the
second floor, which I later learned was Xana Kernodle's room. Just before this
room there was a bathroom door on the south wall of the hallway. As I approached
the room, I could see a body, later identified as Kernodle's, laying on the
floor. Kernodle was laying on her back with her feet facing the east and her
head was facing west. Kernodle had thong-type underwear on and a gray
sweatshirt. There was a large amount of blood on the floor around Kernodle as
well. I could see numerous wounds on Kernodle's arms, hands and face which
appeared to have been caused by an edged weapon. Just next to Kernodle on the
west wall was a small white night stand which appeared to have been moved as it
was pushed against the wall at an odd angle. There was blood on this night stand
in various locations.

The bed in Kernodle's room was situated on the south wall of the bedroom with
the head of the bed appearing to be towards the west wall. I could see a male,
later identified as Ethan Chapin, laying on his back in the bed with some type
of blanket pulled over his mid section. Chapin's body was at an angle on the bed
so that his head was up against the south wall and his feet were hanging off the
bed on the north side. I could not clearly see the wounds to Chapin's head and
neck area but could see a very large amount of blood on the bed as well as what
appeared to be blood spatter on the wall next to Chapin's head and neck. I also
noticed two large cuts to the back of Chapin's legs. The wounds appeared to be
deep into the tissue but were difficult to see due to Ethan's body position.
OFC Smith then took me through the house and pointed out D     M
bedroom which was situated on the southeast side of the second floor of the
house. M           bedroom door opened to the kitchen area as well as the
staircase which led to the third floor of the house. Upon my brief visual
inspection, M           room was disheveled but did not appear to have any blood
or evidence of a struggle within.

I then followed OFC Smith upstairs to the third floor of the residence. The
third floor consisted of two bedrooms and one bathroom. The bedroom on the west
side of the floor was later determined to be Kaylee Goncalves' room. This room
appeared neat and picked up. The only thing of note was the sheets on the bed
were pulled back in a manner which suggested someone had been laying in the bed
and then gotten out of it. OFC Smith then pointed out a small bathroom on the
east side of the third floor. This bathroom shared a wall with Maddison Mogen's
bedroom which was situated on the southeast corner of the third floor.
As I entered this bedroom, I could see two females in the single bed in the
room. The bed was situated along the north wall of the bedroom and both females,
later identified as Mogen and Goncalves, were laying on their backs with their
heads towards the east and their feet towards the west which was also the
direction of the only door into the room. Both Goncalves and Mogen had numerous
visible stab wounds and were covered in a significant amount of blood. I also
later noticed what appeared to be a tan leather knife sheath laying on the bed
next to Mogen's right side (when viewed from the door). I remained in the room
for a moment and then proceeded back outside.
I remained on scene and conducted several more thorough walk-throughs of the
scene with the ISP forensic team, the Latah County Coroner and the Latah County
Prosecutor. Upon receipt of the search warrant prepared by OFC Nunes, the ISP
forensic team lead by Det. Sergeant Darren Gilbertson, processed the scene and
collected an initial fifty-six pieces of evidence. The ISP team turned these
items over to me that evening and I transported them to the MPD temporary
evidence storage. On the morning of 11/14/22, I booked the items into MPD
evidence with the help of evidence custodian T   S          .
