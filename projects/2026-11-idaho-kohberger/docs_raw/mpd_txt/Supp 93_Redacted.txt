3.        Narrative

On November 21, 2022, I traveled from Moscow Idaho to Meridian Idaho to drop off
evidence at the Idaho State Police Forensic Laboratory located at
               Meridian Idaho.
After getting evidence checked into the lab I signed for a DNA profile which had
been generated by the ISP Forensic Lab. This DNA profile was collected from a
knife sheath which was found by ISP investigators from District 2 during the
initial search of 1122 King Road on November 13th. The sheath had been
transported to the lab by ISP troopers.
The DNA profile was stored in a vile which was then packaged in an evidence
envelope by ISP lab technicians and properly sealed with evidence tape. I
signed the package showing I was taking possession of it on 11-21-22 sometime
between 1630 and 1700 hours Mountain Time. Once I took possession of the DNA
profile it stayed in the box provided to me by the ISP lab until 11-22-22 at
around 0330 hours when I removed it from the box and placed it in small
backpack. The backpack then stayed in my possession. The only time it was not
physically holding the backpack was when I went through TSA security at the
Boise Airport. I was able to see the backpack as it went through the security
system and subsequent check of my carry on by TSA security. The envelope the
DNA sample was in never left the backpack at anytime during the security check
by TSA.
