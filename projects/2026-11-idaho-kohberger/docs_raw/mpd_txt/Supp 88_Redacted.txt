Video Capture

Case # 22-M09903
Date: 12/22/2022

On December 22, 2022, I was tasked with re-installing the exterior camera, known
as the "Lightbulb Camera" at      King Road, to capture some comparison video
and audio. I contacted one of the tenants, T       L  , who gave me permission
to access her residence and conduct the recording.
Upon arrival, I unplugged the power to the current router and removed the camera
from the light socket on the exterior of the residence. I then powered on the
router I had secured from the residence earlier and installed the camera I
secured as evidence earlier into the light socket. While installing the camera,
I hear a crack and felt the lightbulb base mount become lose. It should be noted
the approximate temperature outside while installing the camera was between 5
and 9 degrees.
I previously had installed a new SDCard into the camera and powered on the
router and camera at the Moscow Police Department to ensure the camera was
functional.
I then was able to monitor the camera using the Homeye Pro app on my phone. Cpl
Brett Payne was able to drive a white colored vehicle into the view of the
camera. Sgt Dustin Blaker parked a vehicle near the residence and closed the
doors and trunk on a vehicle for audio comparison. A K9 Unit from Lewis County
Sheriff's Office was utilized to capture barking audio comparison from the
residence.

Upon completion of the data collection, I powered off the evidentiary router and
removed the camera from the light socket. In doing so, the base detached from
the camera and a wire became disconnected. I gathered the router and camera,
placed them back into their evidentiary bags and returned them to the secure
computer forensic lab.

I reinstalled the current camera and reconnected the power to the new router. I
contacted L    to ensure she could access her camera, and she responded in the
affirmative. Cpl Payne and I then ensured the residence was locked and left the
residence without further incident.

Upon returning to the Moscow Police Department, I was able to download the
videos collected and they were saved to the server located in the secure
computer forensic lab.
