3.        Narrative

On Friday, April 12, 2024 I met with K   D     at the Moscow Police Department
reference a tip D     had called in during this investigation wherein D      said
he was with some of the victims the night before the homicides and wished to
provide his whereabouts. I recorded my interview with D     and burned them to a
CD which I booked into MPD evidence. The following is a summary of my interview
with D    .
I began by asking D     to tell me what his background with the victims was.
D     told me he hung out with Kernodle frequently during his freshman year of
college prior to Kernodle dating Chapin. D     told me he was in the circle of
friends with the other victims and causally saw F     in the fall of 2022. D
later told me he spent the night with F     on approximately three prior
occasions. I asked D     if he remembered any of the victims telling him about
any strange occurrences. He told me he did not. I asked him if he went to any of
the parties at 1122 King Road. D     told me he did and that there were roughly
twenty people at the parties when he went. I asked D     if he and the other
party attendees would have noticed someone unwelcome in the party. D     told me
they would have and that did not occur at any point when he was there.
I asked D     to tell me what he remembered about what he did the day prior to
the homicides. D     told me he went to 1122 King Road the morning of 11/12/22
at approximately 1000 hours to have a couple of drinks with the residents prior
to going to the football game. D     said they all went to "The Whites" for a
party prior to going to the football tailgate. D     said they left that party
and went to the football tailgate at half time of the game (he was unsure of
what time) and stayed out in the parking lot. I asked D     what he did after
the tailgate. He told me the victims all left, and he went back to
       (his fraternity). D    said he ate some food and went to sleep for the
night.
I asked D     what he remembered about the next morning. D     said a fraternity
brother woke him up around noon to check on him since he had stayed at 1122 King
Road prior and the Vandal Alert had been issued for homicides there. D     said
from then on, he and his fraternity brothers stayed in the house together until
they all left town the next day. I asked him if F     told him anything about
what happened. He said she did not and he did not ask F     about it.

I asked D     if there was anything else he thought I should know and he told me
there was not. I thanked D     for his time, provided him my business card and
asked him to call me with any further concerns or questions.
