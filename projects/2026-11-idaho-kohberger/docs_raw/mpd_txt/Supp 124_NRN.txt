Friends and Family Area Canvass:

On November 20, 2022, multiple friends and family of the victims gathered in
Moscow, organized into several groups, and canvassed several areas in Moscow for
possible evidence.
I was contacted by several people, from the groups, when they located something
of interest. I met with the groups and photographed and collected
the following items:
- A clear food service grade plastic glove, located at the top of the hillside,
to the west of the walking path in between Nez Perce and the U of I
Administration building.

Just down from where this plastic glove was located was a large, beige, metal
Generac generator box. There was a plastic bag, inside of the box, which looked
like it had white grease rags inside of it. I photographed this item and did
not collect it.
- A light pink/beige pair of women's underwear, Warners brand. This was located
in a juniper shrub by the Shattuck Amphitheater in the Charles Houston Shattuck
Arboretum.

- A black winter glove, one size fits all. This items was located near the
steps which lead from Memorial Gym parking lot 39 up to the tennis courts.

The items I collected were transported to MPD, stored in a temporary storage in
Detectives so they could dry, and were later booked into MPD evidence.

One group located what they believed to be blood spots on several rocks in the
Arboretum and Botanical Garden, near the gathering place on the north side near
the kiosk. Det. Talbott, with ISP, tested the spots and determined they were
not blood.
One group located what they believed to be blood on the stairs at building 5 at
the Grove Apartments. Det. Talbott determined this was not blood.
The photos I took were copied to a DVD and booked into MPD evidence.
